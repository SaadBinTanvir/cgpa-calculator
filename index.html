Formula

BRAC University calculates GPA using grade points weighted by course credits.

CGPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ(Credit Hours)

The displayed result is rounded to two decimal places using half-up rounding: a third decimal digit of 5 or greater increases the second decimal digit by 1.

Because this calculator uses every included course's credit and grade point, you do not need to enter an existing CGPA. The calculator derives the CGPA directly from the underlying course records.
